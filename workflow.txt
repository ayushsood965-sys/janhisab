1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & PURPOSE (WHAT IS JANAUDIT & WHY DOES IT EXIST?)
====================================================================================================

WHAT IS JANAUDIT?
JanAudit ("Jan" = People in Hindi, "Audit" = Verification / Accountability) is a full-stack, real-time
civic-technology platform designed specifically for Indian democracy. It bridges the deep communication
and accountability chasm between 1.4 billion Indian citizens and their elected representatives
(Members of Parliament - MPs, Members of Legislative Assembly - MLAs, and Municipal Corporators), as well
as local administrative bodies (PWD, Municipal Corporations, Health Departments, Police Stations, and
Discoms).

WHY WAS IT BUILT? (THE PROBLEM IN INDIAN GOVERNANCE):
1. The 5-Year Amnesia: Politicians make grand election manifestos during rallies, but after election
   day, citizens have no central place to track which promises were actually delivered vs which were
   abandoned ("Jumlas").
2. The Broken Grievance Loop: When a road has dangerous potholes, streetlights don't work, or a civil
   hospital lacks basic medicines, citizen complaints on social media get lost, ignored, or drowned out
   by IT-cell political noise.
3. Information Asymmetry: The average voter does not know their MP/MLA's attendance in Parliament, their
   criminal court cases, how they spent their ₹5 Crore/year MPLAD/MLALAD development funds, or how their
   declared assets grew by 500% in five years.
4. RTI Bureaucracy: The Right to Information (RTI) Act 2005 is one of India's greatest democratic tools,
   but ordinary citizens find the legal drafting intimidating and do not know where to send applications.
5. IT-Cell Manipulation & Brigading: Online rating systems often get hijacked by paid political bots
   who spam 5-star or 1-star reviews without ever setting foot in the constituency.

HOW JANAUDIT SOLVES THIS:
JanAudit acts as an open, democratic "Glasshouse of Governance." It combines crowd-sourced evidence,
un-tamperable mathematical rating algorithms (Quadratic Voting), verified official government gazettes,
instant legal RTI drafting, and gamified citizen audits to make public service transparent, measurable,
and responsive.

2. HIGH-LEVEL PLATFORM ARCHITECTURE & TECH STACK
====================================================================================================

The JanAudit system is built using modern, fast, and secure web technologies:

1. FRONTEND (The Visual Interface in your Web Browser):
   - Framework: React 18 with Vite (ultra-fast rendering, instant page transitions).
   - Styling: Tailwind CSS & custom design tokens (modern typography, high-contrast dark/light accents,
     glassmorphism cards, interactive badges).
   - Routing: React Router v7 (smooth navigation between public feeds, maps, and private dashboards).
   - Real-Time WebSockets: Socket.IO Client (live comment feeds, real-time vote reaction counters,
     Andolan live chats, and instant score updates).
   - Interactive GIS Maps: Leaflet & React-Leaflet (interactive OpenStreetMap visualizer for constituency
     boundaries, pothole pin-drops, and infrastructure issues).
   - Charts & Analytics: Chart.js & React-Chartjs-2 (radar charts for multi-dimensional leader ratings,
     trend lines, attendance vs national average bar graphs).
   - Animations: Framer Motion, Canvas Confetti, and Lottie animations.

2. BACKEND (The Core Server & Logic Brain):
   - Server Engine: Node.js with Express.js REST API.
   - Database: MongoDB with Mongoose Object Data Modeling (ODM). Includes an automatic In-Memory
     MongoDB fallback for zero-configuration local development.
   - Real-Time Engine: Socket.IO WebSocket Server managing live rooms, live ticker broadcasts, and
     ephemeral protest rooms.
   - Authentication & Encryption: JSON Web Tokens (JWT) stored in HTTP headers, Bcrypt password hashing
     (10 salt rounds), and Node Crypto 256-bit random tokens for email/password resets.
   - Image & File Sanitization: Multer file uploads paired with `Sharp` image processing. Sharp
     automatically strips all GPS EXIF metadata, camera serial numbers, and device fingerprints to protect
     whistleblowers' real-world safety.
   - Security Armor: Helmet HTTP security headers, Express Rate Limit (DDoS and spam protection), CORS
     whitelist enforcement, and strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) middlewares.

4. AUTHENTICATION & IDENTITY LIFECYCLE (SIGNUP, VERIFICATION, PASSWORDS, PRIVACY)
====================================================================================================

1. REGISTRATION (/api/auth/signup & AuthModal):
   - A user signs up with their Full Name, chosen or generated Username/Handle, Email, Password, Role,
     State, and Constituency.
   - Pseudonymous Username Generator: If a citizen wishes to remain completely anonymous, the system can
     generate a randomized civic pseudonym (e.g., `PotholeHunter_4892`, `RtiWarrior_1034`, `AngryAloo_8812`).
   - Real-Time Username Availability Checker (/api/auth/check-username): As the user types their desired
     username, the system checks in real time if it is taken. If taken, it automatically suggests 4 smart
     alternatives (e.g. `handle_audit`, `handle_nagrik`, `real_handle`, `handle542`).
   - Zero Plaintext Phone Retention: Phone numbers are hashed using irreversible SHA-256 for Sybil-prevention
     without storing the citizen's personal phone number on the server.

2. CRYPTOGRAPHIC 24-HOUR EMAIL VERIFICATION (/verify-email?token=...):
   - Upon signup, an account is created with `verificationStatus: 'PENDING_EMAIL_VERIFICATION'`.
   - The backend generates a secure 32-byte hexadecimal random token with a 24-hour expiration timestamp.
   - An HTML email is dispatched with a one-click verification link. (In local development mode without SMTP,
     the link is cleanly printed into the terminal console).
   - Once the user clicks the link, their email is marked verified:
     * Citizens transition immediately to `VERIFIED` status.
     * Representatives/Moderators transition to `PENDING_ADMIN_VERIFICATION` awaiting credential audit.

3. FORGOT & RESET PASSWORD WORKFLOW (/reset-password?token=...):
   - If a user forgets their password, they submit their email at `/api/auth/forgot-password`.
   - A secure 1-hour reset token is generated and emailed.
   - The user opens `/reset-password?token=...`, enters their new password (minimum 6 characters), and the
     backend encrypts the new password using bcrypt before invalidating the used token.

4. LOGIN & SESSION MANAGEMENT (/api/auth/login & /api/auth/me):
   - Users can log in using either their Email or their Username Handle + Password.
   - If their account is locked by Super Admin, login is rejected with an explanatory notice.
   - Upon successful login, the system issues a signed JWT Token (30-day validity) and awards the user
     their Daily Active Streak bonus (+15 Janta Points).

5. ₹11 UPI "VERIFIED NAGRIK" TRUST BADGE (/api/auth/verify-nagrik-upi):
   - To combat fake bot accounts without requiring invasive government Aadhaar ID storage, JanAudit
     introduces a voluntary ₹11 UPI micro-verification trust mechanism.
   - Completing this simulation unlocks the "Verified Nagrik" shield badge on the user's profile, awards
     +150 Karma Points, +300 Janta Points, and boosts their civic credibility tier.

6. THE DYNAMIC IMPACT SCORE™ & DIVERGENCE ENGINE (HOW POLITICIANS ARE RATED)
====================================================================================================

Unlike subjective opinion polls or partisan television debates, JanAudit rates every elected representative
(MP/MLA) on an objective, auditable, mathematical scale from 0.0 to 100.0 known as the Impact Score™.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE 4-PILLAR MATHEMATICAL FORMULA:
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Impact Score = (Pillar 1 * 45%) + (Pillar 2 * 25%) + (Pillar 3 * 20%) + (Pillar 4 * 10%)

PILLAR 1: OBJECTIVE PARLIAMENTARY & ECI DATA (45% WEIGHT)
Data sourced directly from official records (Digital Sansad, PRS Legislative Research, MyNeta/ADR affidavits):
1. Legislative Attendance (15%): Actual days attended in Parliament/Assembly vs national average (79%).
2. Questions Asked & Debates (15%): Number of public interest queries raised on the assembly floor.
3. Criminal Record Penalty (20% Negative Penalty):
   - 0 pending cases = 100 / 100 score.
   - Each pending criminal charge docks 20 points.
   - Each conviction docks 45 points.
4. Asset Growth vs Market Benchmark (15%):
   - Compares the politician's 5-year declared asset growth with the Sensex/economic benchmark (approx 74%).
   - Asset growth > 200% yields 70/100; > 500% yields 45/100; > 1000% yields 20/100 (Unexplained Wealth Penalty).
5. Private Member Bills Introduced (10%): Proactivity in formulating new laws.
6. Fund Utilization (15%): Percentage of ₹5 Cr/year MPLADS or MLALADS development budget spent on local works.
7. RTI Compliance Rate (10%): History of government departments under their charge answering citizen RTIs on time.

PILLAR 2: VERIFIED LOCAL OUTCOMES & GROUND TRUTH (25% WEIGHT)
- Number of delivered election manifesto promises (from the Promise Tracker).
- Volume of community-corroborated, geotagged evidence files uploaded by verified local citizens.

PILLAR 3: COMMUNITY SENTIMENT & QUADRATIC CITIZEN RATINGS (20% WEIGHT)
- Citizens rate the representative across 7 dimensions (Infrastructure, Accessibility, Promise Keeping,
  Transparency, Legislative, Social, Economic) on a 0-100 slider.
- Anti-Manipulation Protections applied:
  a) Quadratic Cost: To cast N votes, a user must spend N^2 Janta Points (1 vote=1pt, 2 votes=4pts, 3 votes=9pts).
     This stops rich users or bot accounts from skewing the results.
  b) Evidence Multiplier: Ratings submitted with attached proof receive a 3x weight boost (Tier 1 proof) or 1.5x boost (Tier 2).
  c) Geographic Local Multiplier: Verified voters residing inside the politician's actual constituency receive 3.0x rating weight.
  d) Wilson Lower Bound Confidence: Statistical adjustment that prevents a politician with only 1 positive vote from getting 100%.

PILLAR 4: TRUST RECENCY DECAY (10% WEIGHT)
- Rewards consistent year-round public engagement while penalizing "Missing in Action" politicians who only show
  up 2 months before elections.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE 6 BADGE TIERS:
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Based on the final computed Impact Score (0 to 100), every politician is assigned a public badge:

Score 90.0 - 100.0: 🌟 "Janta ka Sher" ("The Chaiwala Who Delivered") — Exemplary public service
Score 70.0 - 89.9:  ✅ "Kaam Karne Wala" ("Actually Does Stuff") — Solid positive governance
Score 50.0 - 69.9:  😐 "Theek Hai" ("Suspiciously Silent") — Average, uninspiring performance
Score 30.0 - 49.9:  😴 "Sust Neta" ("Missing Action Hero") — High absenteeism, stalled projects
Score 10.0 - 29.9:  🤥 "Jumla Champion" ("Ghotala Specialist") — Unfulfilled promises, criminal charges
Score 0.0 - 9.9:    🤡 "Total Nautanki" ("Janta ka Dushman") — Extreme negligence, critical public failure

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE DIVERGENCE ENGINE (ROTTEN TOMATOES DUAL-SCORE SYSTEM):
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Just like movie reviews show a Critic Score vs Audience Score, JanAudit calculates:
- Kaam Score (Objective Data Score from official government records)
- Janta Voice (Ground Citizen Sentiment Score)

If the difference between Kaam Score and Janta Voice exceeds 30 points, the system flags a Divergence:
1. ⚠️ SUSPICIOUS HIGH SENTIMENT: If public ratings are 90% but objective data shows 0 attendance and 0 funds spent,
   JanAudit flags "Possible coordinated rating bot activity under review."
2. ℹ️ UNPOPULAR HIGH DELIVERY: If a politician has 95% attendance and finished all roads, but has low sentiment due
   to partisan noise, JanAudit highlights "Strong legislative delivery amidst intense public scrutiny."

reports, investigations, and community polls.

- Post Types: Citizens can publish Text grievances, Memes, Evidence documents, Videos, Audio recordings,

- Evidence Level Badges:
  * 🟢 Verified: Supported by official RTI copies, court orders, or corroborated geotagged photos.

  golden "Seal of Janta" shield.
- Custom Political Reactions:
  Instead of generic social media likes, citizens react with:
  🔥 (Viral Expose / Fire) | 💀 (Dead Governance) | 😂 (Meme / Comedy) | 🤡 (Jumla Alert) | ✊ (Solidarity) | 🧾 (Needs Proof)
- Automated EXIF Stripping:
  All uploaded grievance photos are automatically re-processed through the server's `Sharp` engine,
  erasing the photographer's exact phone GPS coordinates and device model to guarantee complete physical safety.
- Janta Points Boost:
  Users can spend their Janta Points (e.g. 50 XP) to boost important local reports to the top of the feed.

9. SECURITY, PRIVACY & ANTI-MANIPULATION DEFENSES
====================================================================================================

JanAudit implements a defense-in-depth security model to safeguard democratic discourse:

1. ZERO-PII CRYPTOGRAPHIC STORAGE:
   - Plaintext mobile phone numbers are never stored in the database. Phone numbers are converted into
     one-way SHA-256 cryptographic hashes for Sybil-resistance.
2. EXIF METADATA STRIPPING:
   - Every uploaded image is scrubbed by the `Sharp` image processing engine, stripping GPS coordinates,
     camera model, and timestamp metadata so whistleblowers cannot be tracked.
3. QUADRATIC VOTING ARMOR:
   - Standard voting allows wealthy entities to purchase thousands of bot votes. Quadratic voting increases
     the cost of casting additional votes exponentially (Cost = N^2), making mass brigade manipulation
     mathematically prohibitive.
4. GEOGRAPHIC 3X RELEVANCE MULTIPLIER:
   - Ratings cast by verified local voters living within the politician's actual constituency receive 3x the
     mathematical weight compared to outside accounts.
5. DIVERGENCE ASTROTURF DETECTION:
   - If a politician with 0 attendance and high criminal records suddenly receives a flood of 5-star reviews,
     the Divergence Engine triggers a public alert warning citizens of astroturfing.
6. RATE LIMITING & SECURITY HEADERS:
   - `express-rate-limit` prevents brute-force login attempts and spam grievance flooding.
   - `helmet` enforces strict HTTP Content-Security-Policy and cross-site scripting (XSS) protections.
